A fresh Flagpost install ships with **no administrator account** — there is
no default password to change, because there is no default login to leak.
The first visit is greeted by the **setup wizard**, which creates the owner
account and the initial branding; until an administrator exists, public
registration stays closed and the site reports itself as unconfigured.

Your administrator account is global: it carries the site-wide permissions
this guide covers — users, roles, sign-in options, site settings, and every
competition on the instance. Day-to-day *event* work (authoring challenges,
running a competition) is the Judge guide; many admins wear both hats, but
the surfaces are separate.

## Your home: the Admin area

![The admin overview](assets/overview.png)
*Admin → Overview: the site at a glance — accounts, competitions, and
activity across the instance.*

The **Admin** section in the sidebar gathers the global surfaces: Overview,
Users, Roles, Competitions, Pages, Automations, the audit log (Events), and
Site settings. Each gets a chapter or section below.

!!! note "Delegate with roles, not shared logins"
    Resist the shared admin account. Chapter 3 shows how to grant a
    colleague exactly the site-wide permissions they need under their own
    identity — the audit log is only as useful as the names in it.
