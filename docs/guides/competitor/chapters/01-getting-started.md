Flagpost is the platform your organisers use to run their capture-the-flag:
it hosts the challenges, grades your flags and your code, runs the scoreboard,
and connects you with the event staff. Each chapter stands on its own when
you need it mid-event — and the guide is always one click away in the app,
under **User guides** in the top bar.

## Your account

Your **username is your identity**: it's what you sign in with and what the
scoreboard shows. An email address is optional unless your organisers require
one — adding it is what lets you reset a forgotten password.

Accounts arrive one of three ways: **register yourself** (if public
registration is open — some sites limit it to certain email domains), **sign
in with an identity provider** (a **Sign in with …** button — Google,
Microsoft, GitHub, Discord or your organisation's SSO; the account is created
on first use), or **receive credentials** from the organisers. A directory
(LDAP) account signs in through the normal username and password form.

## Signing in

![The sign-in page](assets/login.png){: .narrow }
*Sign in with your username or email — or any identity-provider button your
site offers.*

Forgotten password? **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page emails a reset
link — provided your account has an email address.

!!! note "Email verification"
    Some sites require a verified email before you can **join** a
    competition. Signing in still works; a blocked join sends you to your
    Profile, where **Resend verification email** gets you a fresh link.

## Your profile

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open **Profile**:

- **Account** — your **profile picture** (PNG, JPEG or WebP), **username**
  (if your site allows self-service renames: once every 30 days, confirmed
  with your password), **password**, **email**, and any **registration
  details** an event asked for.
- **Notifications** — what reaches your bell, desktop notifications, sound
  (chapter 7).
- **Skills** — the categories you've solved in, across every event on this
  site.
- **API tokens** — for scripts that talk to Flagpost directly (chapter 8).
- **Certificates** — participation certificates organisers release.

The top bar holds **Language** (English, French, Spanish or Polish,
remembered by this browser) and **Theme** (a dark or light palette, or **Use
site default**).

!!! tip "One account, many events"
    Your account belongs to the site, not to one competition. The next
    event on the same instance just needs you to join it — chapter 2.
