Flagpost is the platform your organisers use to run their capture-the-flag:
it hosts the challenges, grades your flags, runs the scoreboard, and connects
you with the event staff. This guide covers everything you need as a
competitor — each chapter stands on its own when you need it mid-event.

## Your account

Your **username is your identity**: it's what you sign in with and what the
scoreboard shows. An email address is optional unless your organisers require
one — adding it lets you reset a forgotten password and receive email
notifications.

Accounts arrive one of three ways: **register yourself** (if public
registration is open — some sites restrict it to certain email domains or
require verification), **sign in with an identity provider** (Google,
Microsoft, GitHub, Discord, or your organisation's SSO — the account is
created on first use), or **receive credentials** from the organisers.

## Signing in

![The sign-in page](assets/login.png){: .narrow }
*Sign in with your username or email — or any identity-provider button your
site offers.*

Forgotten password? Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page (needs an
email on file).

!!! tip "One account, many events"
    Your account belongs to the site, not to one competition. When the next
    event runs on the same instance, sign in as usual and join it.

## Your profile

From the avatar menu you can change your **password** or **email**, pick a
personal **theme palette**, and manage **API tokens** if you use tooling that
talks to Flagpost directly.

If you sign in before joining any competition you'll land in the **lobby** —
the list of competitions you can join. That's chapter 2.
