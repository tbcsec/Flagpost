Flagpost is the platform your event's organisers use to run their capture-the-flag
competition — it hosts the challenges, grades your flags, tracks the scoreboard,
and connects you with the organisers while you play. This guide covers
everything you need as a competitor. You don't need any of it memorised;
each chapter stands on its own when you need it mid-event.

## Your account

Your **username is your identity** on Flagpost. It's the name you sign in
with, the name on the scoreboard, and it's unique on the site (ignoring
case). An email address is optional unless your organisers require one —
adding it lets you reset a forgotten password yourself and receive email
notifications.

Depending on how the site is configured, you'll get an account one of three
ways:

- **Register yourself** on the sign-up page, if public registration is open.
  Some sites restrict registration to specific email domains, or ask you to
  verify your address before signing in.
- **Sign in with an identity provider** — the login page may offer buttons
  such as Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Discord, or your organisation's own
  single sign-on. Your account is created automatically the first time you
  use one.
- **Receive credentials** from the organisers.

## Signing in

Sign in with your **username or email** and password, or with any identity
provider button shown. If you've forgotten your password and have an email on
file, use **Forgot password** on the login page.

!!! tip "One account, many events"
    Your account belongs to the site, not to one competition. When the next
    event runs on the same instance, you sign in with the same account and
    simply join the new competition.

## Your profile

Open your profile from the avatar menu to:

- change your **password** or **email address**,
- pick a personal **theme palette** for the interface,
- manage **API tokens**, if you use tooling that talks to Flagpost directly.

## The lobby

If you sign in before joining any competition, you'll land in the **lobby** —
a simple page listing the competitions you can join. Chapter 2 covers what
happens from there.
