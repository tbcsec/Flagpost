A Flagpost site can host many competitions. Each one you join gets its own
challenges, scoreboard, and team — switch between competitions you belong to
with the selector in the top bar.

## Finding your competition

- **Open competitions** appear in the lobby with a **Join** button.
- **Invite-only competitions** need an **invite code** from your organisers —
  enter it in the lobby's *join by code* field.

## Rules and code of conduct

Many events ask you to **accept the competition rules** before you can join,
and again if the organisers change the rules mid-event. Until you accept,
gameplay is blocked — read them; they're binding for prizes and conduct
decisions.

## Individual or team mode

Every competition is configured as one of:

| Mode | You compete as | Scoreboard shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | yourself | players |
| **Team** | a team you create or join | teams |

In a team competition you'll be prompted to **create a team** or **join one
with a team code** after joining. Your teammates share solves, points, hints,
and a private scratchpad on every challenge — chapter 5 covers team play in
detail.

!!! note "Brackets"
    Some events divide entrants into **brackets** — for example a student
    division and a professional division. If your competition uses them,
    you'll be placed in one (or asked to choose), and the scoreboard can be
    filtered per bracket. Prizes are typically awarded within a bracket.

## Before the start

Joining early is normal and encouraged. Until the organisers **start** the
competition, the challenges page shows a "hasn't started yet" notice — the
challenge list, and with it the scoreboard, opens the moment the event goes
live. If the schedule is published you'll see the start time on the
competition's pages.

Once the competition **ends**, flag submissions close automatically. The
scoreboard and your solve history remain visible.
