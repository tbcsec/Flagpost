A Flagpost site can host many competitions, each with its own challenges,
scoreboard, and teams. Switch between the ones you belong to with the
selector in the top bar.

## Finding your competition

**Open competitions** appear in the lobby with a Join button. **Invite-only
competitions** need an **invite code** from your organisers — enter it in the
lobby's *join by code* field.

## Rules first

Many events ask you to **accept the rules** before you can play — and again
if they change mid-event. Gameplay is blocked until you do. Read them:
they're binding for prizes and conduct decisions.

## Individual or team mode

| Mode | You compete as | Scoreboard shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | yourself | players |
| **Team** | a team you create or join | teams |

In a team competition you'll be prompted to **create a team** or **join one
with a team code**. Teammates share solves, points, hints, and a private
scratchpad on every challenge (chapter 5).

!!! note "Brackets"
    Some events split entrants into divisions — say, students and
    professionals. If yours does, you'll be placed in a bracket, the
    scoreboard can filter by it, and prizes are typically awarded within it.

## Start and finish

Joining early is normal. Until the organisers **start** the event, the
challenges page shows a "hasn't started yet" notice — challenges and
scoreboard open the moment it goes live. When the competition **ends**,
submissions close automatically; the scoreboard and your history stay
visible.
