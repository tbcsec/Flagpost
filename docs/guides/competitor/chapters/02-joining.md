A Flagpost site can host many competitions, each with its own challenges,
scoreboard and teams. The selector in the top bar switches between the ones
you've joined.

## Finding your competition

Most organisers send an **invite link**. Open it and you'll see *You're
invited to …*; sign in or **Create an account** if you need to, and you're
brought straight back to a one-click **Join competition**. An expired or
revoked link just says *This link isn't available*; if the event has ended
or its registration window isn't open, the page tells you so.

Until you've joined your first competition you land in the **Lobby**
instead: **Public competitions** list a **Join** button, and **Join with an
invite code** takes the code your organisers gave you. (This is the
*competition* code — a team's invite code goes on the Participants page,
chapter 6.) To join a later event, use its invite link, pick it under
**Open to join** in the competition switcher and press **Join competition**,
or press **Join** beside the switcher (a **+** on a phone) to get back to
the lobby.

Some events ask for **a few details** as you join — affiliation, shirt size,
dietary needs. Required ones must be filled before **Join** enables; change
your answers later under Profile → Account → **Registration details**.

## Rules first

Many events make you **accept the rules** before you can play — tick *I have
read and accept these rules* and **Accept** — and again if the organisers
change this competition's rules. Some only display them (**Continue**). Read
them either way: they're binding for prizes and conduct decisions.

## Individual or team mode

| Mode | You compete as | Scoreboard shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | yourself | players |
| **Team** | a team you create or join | teams |

In a team competition nothing prompts you — open **Participants** in the
sidebar and either **Create a team** (you become its captain) or **Join a
team** with a teammate's invite code. You can't submit anything until you're
on a team. Chapter 6 has the details.

!!! note "Divisions"
    Some events split entrants into divisions — say, students and
    professionals. Organisers assign yours; the scoreboard's **Division**
    filter then ranks you within it, and prizes are typically awarded per
    division.

## Start and finish

Joining early is normal. Until the organisers **start** the event, the
Challenges page shows *This competition hasn't started yet* — challenges and
the scoreboard open the moment it goes live. When the competition **ends**,
the Challenges page closes entirely (challenge text, files and notes
included), while the scoreboard stays up as **Final standings** and your
Dashboard keeps your solves.
