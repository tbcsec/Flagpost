## Hints

A hint is either a **Free hint** or **Costs N pts**. **Reveal** charges
immediately — no confirmation — and deducts the cost from your (or your
team's) score, so agree before spending. Revealed hints stay visible all
event, organisers can release extra ones as the competition unfolds, and
hints close while the competition is paused.

## How points work

| Scheme | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Static** | Fixed value — solve any time for the same points. |
| **Dynamic** | The value **decays as more people solve it**, from a starting value toward a floor. Every solver holds the *current* value, first or last. |

Dynamic scoring rewards hard challenges naturally: fewer solvers, higher
worth — the card always shows the current value. **First blood** — the first
solver — is marked on the challenge's solver list; some events attach prizes
to it.

Your total is your solve points, minus hint costs, plus or minus any awards
or adjustments the organisers make — never below zero. Ties go to whoever
made their last solve first.

## The scoreboard

![The scoreboard](assets/scoreboard.png)
*Live standings: the top ten as bars and **Points over time** for the
leaders, with the full table below — your own row is highlighted.*

If your event uses divisions, the **Division** filter ranks within one. Some
events also publish a **public spectator scoreboard** friends can watch
without an account.

!!! note "Scoreboard freezes"
    Many events **freeze** the board near the end to keep the finale
    suspenseful. You'll see a *Frozen* badge and *The scoreboard is frozen*.
    Solves still count during the freeze — standings, including the
    scoreboard you see in Flagpost, just stop updating until the organisers
    unfreeze it. Don't panic when a late solve doesn't appear to move you.

## Rating challenges

If your organisers enable it, *How was this challenge?* appears after a
solve — one to five stars, and you can change your rating later. Authors see
aggregate ratings; it genuinely shapes future events.

## Certificates

Some events issue participation certificates. When the organisers release
them, *Your certificate is ready!* pops up with a **Download** button, the
scoreboard shows a card, and the certificate stays available under Profile →
**Certificates** for every event you've earned one in. Organisers choose
whether everyone or only scoring participants receive one.
