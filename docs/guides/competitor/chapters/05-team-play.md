In team mode your team is the scoring unit: solves, points, hint costs, and
guess caps are shared. (Individual players: the notes below work for you too —
they're simply private to you.)

## Your team

Create a team when you join, or enter your team's **join code**. Any
member's correct flag scores for the whole team, and their solve shows as
solved for everyone — so split the categories and divide the board rather
than double-working challenges. Team names appear on the scoreboard; pick one
you're happy to see at the closing ceremony.

## Shared challenge notes

Every challenge carries a **notes scratchpad** — a live collaborative editor,
private to your team, separate per challenge. Everyone can type at once,
edits merge in real time, and notes persist all event. In individual mode
the same pad is personal and syncs across your devices.

Use it as the team's memory: partial progress, working theories, payloads
that *almost* worked. When someone rotates onto a challenge three hours in,
the pad tells them everything that's been tried.

## Coordinating in practice

1. **Claim loudly** — put "I'm on this" at the top of the pad so two people
   don't burn the same hour.
2. **Dump progress as you go** — commands, endpoints, credentials, dead ends.
3. **Rotate stuck challenges** — fresh eyes beat tired ones; the pad makes
   hand-offs cheap.
4. **Watch the value** — on dynamic scoring, sometimes the winning move is to
   bank easier points first.
