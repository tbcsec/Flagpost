## Announcements & notifications

Organisers broadcast **announcements** for anything event-wide — new
challenge waves, hints, schedule changes. They arrive live as a banner, and
the full history stays in the feed: glance at it after any break. The
**bell** collects notifications addressed to you — ticket replies, team
events — with an unread badge.

## Support tickets

When something's wrong — a target seems down, a sure flag is rejected,
you're out of guesses, or you have a rules question — open a ticket from the
**Support** page.

![Support](assets/support.png)
*Each ticket is a live conversation with the organisers — replies appear in
real time, and you're notified when they answer.*

Name the challenge, say exactly what you tried, and keep one issue per
ticket so each can be resolved cleanly.

!!! tip "Ask early"
    Judges would far rather field a quick "is this challenge behaving?" than
    have you lose an hour to a broken target. You may be the first to notice.

## Custom pages

Organisers often publish extra pages — full rules, prizes, venue or sponsor
info. Look for them in the sidebar under the event's name.

## Conduct

The rules you accepted are the contract. The universal baseline: attack only
the challenge infrastructure you're pointed at — never the platform or other
competitors; don't share flags or solutions outside your team while the
event runs; report anything that looks like an unintended shortcut.
Organisers can see submission patterns — play it straight.

Good luck, and enjoy the event. 🚩
