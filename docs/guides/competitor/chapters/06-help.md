## Announcements

Organisers broadcast announcements for anything event-wide: new challenge
waves, hint releases, schedule changes, infrastructure notices. They arrive
**live** as a banner while you're on the site, and the full history is always
available in the announcements feed. Glance at it after any break — a
mid-event clarification often saves an hour of confusion.

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar collects notifications addressed to **you** —
replies to your support tickets, team events, and anything organisers send
your way. A badge shows unread items.

## Support tickets

When something's wrong — a challenge target seems down, a flag you're sure of
is rejected, you're out of guesses, or you have a rules question — open a
**support ticket** from the Support page:

1. Choose the competition, give the ticket a clear subject, and describe the
   problem. Name the challenge and include exactly what you tried.
2. The ticket becomes a **live conversation** with the organisers — replies
   appear in real time, and you're notified when they answer.
3. Keep one issue per ticket so each can be resolved and closed cleanly.

!!! tip "Ask early"
    Judges would far rather field a quick "is this challenge behaving
    correctly?" than have a team lose an hour to a broken target. If
    something feels off, say so — you may be the first to notice.

## Custom pages

Organisers often publish extra pages for their event — rules in full, prize
details, venue or sponsor information. Look for them in the sidebar under
the event's name.

## Conduct

The competition rules you accepted are the contract for the event. The
universal baseline: attack only the challenge infrastructure you're pointed
at, never the platform or other competitors; don't share flags or solutions
outside your team while the event runs; and report anything that looks like
an unintended shortcut. Organisers can see submission patterns — play it
straight.

Good luck, and enjoy the event. 🚩
