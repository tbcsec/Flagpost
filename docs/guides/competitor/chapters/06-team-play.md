In team mode your team is the scoring unit: solves, points, hint costs,
guess caps and challenge instances are all shared. (Individual players: the
notes below work for you too — they're simply private to you.)

## Your team

Everything happens on the **Participants** page:

- **Create a team** makes you its **captain**. Tick *Require captain
  approval to join* if you want to vet joiners. A team can't be renamed in
  the app once created, so choose the name carefully — it's what the
  scoreboard shows at the closing ceremony.
- **Join a team** takes the **Invite code** a teammate shares from your team
  card. On an approval-required team your request waits for the captain,
  who sees **Join requests** with Approve / Reject.
- The captain can set the **Team profile** — affiliation, country, website.
- **Leave team** hands captaincy to the longest-standing member; the last
  member leaving dissolves the team.

Any member's correct flag or accepted program scores for the whole team and
shows as solved for everyone — so split the categories and divide the board
rather than double-working challenges.

## Team notes

Every flag challenge carries **Team notes** — a live collaborative pad,
private to your team, separate per challenge. Everyone can type at once,
edits merge in real time, and notes persist all event. (Programming
challenges have no notes pad.)

Use it as the team's memory: partial progress, working theories, payloads
that *almost* worked. When someone rotates onto a challenge three hours in,
the pad tells them everything that's been tried.

## Coordinating in practice

1. **Claim loudly** — put "I'm on this" at the top of the pad so two people
   don't burn the same hour.
2. **Dump progress as you go** — commands, endpoints, credentials, dead ends.
3. **Rotate stuck challenges** — fresh eyes beat tired ones; the pad makes
   hand-offs cheap.
4. **Watch the value** — on dynamic scoring, sometimes the winning move is to
   bank easier points first.
5. **One instance per challenge** — the team shares it; agree who launches,
   extends and stops.
