## My flag is right but it was rejected — why?

Flags are graded exactly as submitted: full string, correct case (unless
that challenge was authored case-insensitive), no stray whitespace, and the
complete format — `flag{...}` wrappers included when shown. Paste rather
than retype. Still stuck after checking those? Open a support ticket naming
the challenge and what you submitted.

## A challenge was worth 500 — why is it 380 now?

That's **dynamic scoring**: the value decays as more people solve it, and
every solver holds the current value. It can also be the **wrong-guess
penalty** on a multiple-choice question — each wrong answer reduces what
that question is worth *to you or your team*, shown struck through.

## I solved something but the scoreboard didn't move.

Near the end of an event the public board is often **frozen** — your solve
counted the moment you submitted, and standings update at the reveal.
Mid-event, remember the board ranks totals: a 100-point solve may not
change your rank.

## Can I use Flagpost on my laptop and phone at once?

Yes — sign in on as many devices as you like. Your challenge notes sync
live between them, so findings captured at the venue are on your laptop
when you sit back down.

## What if I forget my password mid-event?

**Forgot password** on the sign-in page emails you a reset link — provided
your account has an email address. Add one in your Profile *now*, before
you need it.

## I'm out of guesses on a multiple-choice question.

Open a support ticket. Organisers can restore attempts for you or your
team — it's non-destructive, so nothing about your history is lost.

## Who can read my challenge notes?

Your team, and only your team — each challenge's pad is private to the
team it belongs to (in individual mode, private to you). Write freely.

## Can my tools talk to Flagpost directly?

Yes — create a personal **API token** in your Profile and use it from
scripts or clients. Tokens act as you, so treat them like your password
and delete any you stop using.
