A judge is **competition-scoped staff**: everything in this guide applies to
the competition(s) you help organise, granted through your role there.
Site-wide concerns — user accounts, sign-in options, branding — belong to
administrators (they have their own guide).

What Flagpost's permissions give you that competitors don't see:

- **Manage buttons** on the surfaces you can author — most importantly
  *Manage challenges* on the challenges page.
- **Drafts** — unpublished challenges are visible to you everywhere,
  labelled, and invisible to competitors.
- **The status-gate bypass** — before the competition starts (and after it
  ends) competitors see a closed notice, while you keep full access to
  build, test, and review. While the event is *paused*, competitors can't
  submit but you still can, so you can verify a fix live.

## Your home: the dashboard

![The staff dashboard](assets/dashboard.png)
*The operational overview: solve/submission counters, live activity,
challenge health (solves vs. attempts), the announcement composer, and the
support queue.*

The dashboard is the place to leave open while the event runs — every widget
updates live, and **Customize** lets you arrange it to taste. The rest of
this guide walks the three phases it fronts: building the event, running it,
and supporting the people playing it.

!!! tip "Custom roles"
    Your role may be narrower than the classic Judge — permissions are
    granular (challenge editing, schedule control, tickets, announcements are
    all separate grants). If a surface in this guide is missing for you, ask
    your administrator which grants your role carries.
