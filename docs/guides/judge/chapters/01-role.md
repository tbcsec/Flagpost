A judge is **competition-scoped staff**: everything in this guide applies to
the competition(s) you help organise, granted through your role there.
Site-wide concerns — user accounts, sign-in options, branding, the judge and
instance infrastructure — belong to administrators, who have their own
guide. This one is a click away in the app, under **User guides** in the top
bar.

What your role gives you that competitors don't see:

- **Manage buttons** on the surfaces you can author — most importantly
  *Manage challenges* on the challenges page.
- **Drafts** — unpublished challenges are visible to you everywhere,
  labelled, and invisible to competitors.
- **The status-gate bypass** — before the competition starts (and after it
  ends) competitors see a closed notice, while anyone who can edit challenges
  keeps full access. While the event is *paused*, competitors can't submit
  but you still can.

!!! note "Your solves are real"
    A staff flag submission is graded like anyone's: in individual mode it
    isn't ranked, but it takes **first blood**, decays a dynamic challenge's
    value and fires automations; in team mode you'd have to join a team
    first. Open and download freely on the live board, but rehearse flag
    submission in a clone — and for programming problems use **Run
    examples** or the reference check, which never score.

## Your home: the dashboard

![The staff dashboard](assets/dashboard.png)
*The operational overview: At a glance, Recent solves, the Announcements
archive, Challenge health and the Support queue — every widget live.*

The header carries **New announcement** and **Customize**, which opens an
edit mode: drag to move or resize, **Add section** to pick from a catalog
(unsolved challenges, difficulty progress, team activity, brute-force watch,
a moderation feed, schedule & status, instance health), then **Save
layout**. Until your first challenge is published, a *Getting started* card
walks the first steps.

## Permissions are granular

Your role may be narrower than the built-in Judge. If a surface is missing
for you, this is what gates it:

| Surface | Grant |
|---|---|
| Settings → Modules / Sharing | manage modules / manage invite links |
| Settings → Certificates / Reports | manage certificates / generate report |
| Start / Stop · Pause · Freeze | manage schedule · edit competition · scoreboard freeze |
| Sidebar: Judging · Instances · AI transcripts | judging view · instance view · AI transcripts |
| Participants → Create award | score override |

Ask your administrator which grants your role carries.
