## Announcements

**New announcement** on the dashboard broadcasts to everyone in the
competition — it lands live as a banner on every open session and stays in
the feed. Use it for challenge waves, hint drops, schedule changes, and
incident notices. Announce *before* competitors notice, not after the
tickets arrive.

## The support queue

![The support queue](assets/tickets.png)
*Tickets are live conversations — replies stream in real time on both
sides, and competitors are notified when you answer.*

Competitor tickets arrive in **Support**, with the open count on your
dashboard's queue widget. Working principles that keep a queue healthy:

- **Acknowledge fast, resolve second** — a one-line "looking into it" stops
  the duplicate tickets and the hallway anxiety.
- **Reproduce before you rule** — "the flag won't submit" is a claim; paste
  it yourself before answering. You can submit even while paused.
- If a challenge really is broken, **pause or unpublish it**, announce, fix,
  and consider the scoring impact before resuming.
- Close resolved tickets so the queue reflects reality.

## Guess resets

Multiple-choice challenges cap guesses. When a competitor exhausts theirs
for a legitimate reason (a broken hint, an ambiguous option you've since
fixed), open the challenge in the editor: its **Guesses** section resets
attempts for one team/competitor or for everyone — history is kept, so
nothing is destroyed.

!!! note "Fairness travels in pairs"
    Anything you grant one team — a reset, a nudge, extra time on a target —
    be prepared to grant every team in the same situation. The audit log
    records staff actions, and post-event write-ups read better when the
    answer to "why them?" is "same as everyone".
