Everything about the competition itself lives in **Settings**, in tabs:
General, Controls, Challenges, Rules, Sharing, Assistant, Certificates,
Reports and Modules (the last five appear with the matching module or
grant).

![Competition settings](assets/settings.png)
*General holds identity, visibility, the public scoreboard and divisions,
with Registration fields below; Controls holds the status, pause and freeze
levers plus the schedule.*

## Before the event

- **General** — name, description, team vs individual mode (with **Max team
  size**), **Visibility** (*Private* or *Public*), **Public scoreboard**
  (lists the event on the site's spectator page), the **CTFtime scoreboard
  feed**, and **Brackets / divisions** — parallel rankings such as Students
  and Open. Below the form, **Registration fields** adds questions
  competitors or team captains answer on joining — text, choose-one or
  checkbox, optionally required — collected for your **Export CSV** and
  never shown publicly.
- **Challenges** — categories, difficulty tiers and tags; the
  **multiple-choice guess limit** and **wrong-guess penalty**; and whether
  competitors are asked to rate challenges.
- **Rules** — a per-competition override of the site's rules and code of
  conduct. Leave it empty to use the site document; tick **Display only** to
  show rules without requiring acceptance. Saving a new or changed override
  re-prompts every participant (**Save and re-prompt**).
- **Sharing** — **invite links**. **New link** mints a *Join* link (which,
  with the permanent invite code, is the only self-serve way into a private
  competition) or a *View* link to the public spectator board, each with an
  optional label, expiry and maximum joins; **Revoke** kills a link for
  good. Join links honour the registration window and stop admitting once
  the event ends.
- **Modules** — the eight optional features: AI Assistants, Analytics,
  Automations, Certificates, Challenge Instances, Code Judge, Feedback &
  Surveys, Post-Event Reports. All start on; switch off what you won't use.
  AI, Instances and Code Judge also need the administrator's site setup
  before they do anything.

## The status gate

A competition is **not started → running → ended**. Competitors can open
challenges and submit only while it's *running* — before and after they see
a closed notice (the final scoreboard stays visible), while staff retain
full access. The **Schedule** at the bottom of Controls — *Starts*, *Ends*,
*Registration opens*, *Registration closes* — drives the transitions and
saves with the form; **Start competition** / **Stop competition** (and
**Re-open**) override it on the spot.

## Mid-event levers (Controls)

- **Gameplay pause** — closes flag submissions, code submissions and hint
  reveals during an incident. The Challenges page shows a paused banner;
  post an announcement too. Staff can still submit, to verify a fix.
- **Scoreboard freeze** — stop standings updates near the finale, in the
  app and on the public board alike. Solves keep counting; staff can switch
  the Scoreboard to **Show live standings**; unfreeze at your ceremony.
  While frozen, your dashboard stops updating on its own — reload it for
  current figures. **Freeze board** is also on the Scoreboard page.

!!! tip "Dry-run before doors open"
    While the competition is not started, open every challenge and download
    every file. Rehearse the *flags* in a clone (ask an administrator) —
    staff solves on the live event are real (chapter 1). And save before
    switching competitions: unsaved Settings edits are discarded.
