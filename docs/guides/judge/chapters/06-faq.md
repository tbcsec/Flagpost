## What do competitors see of a draft?

Nothing — no card, no count, no scoreboard trace. Drafts (including
unpublished-again challenges) exist only for staff, so you can build and
repair openly during a live event.

## I edited a live challenge — what happens to points already scored?

Description, connection info, hints and attachments apply immediately.
Point values: on a **static** challenge, existing solves keep the value
they scored and future solves earn the new one; on a **dynamic** challenge
the platform already re-values every solver as the decay moves. If a
mistake cost teams points, compensate with an **award** rather than
retro-editing values.

## Can I grant bonus points?

Yes — **awards**: titled, point-bearing recognition granted to a team or
competitor (write-up prizes, sportsmanship, compensation for a broken
target). They fold into the scoreboard like any other points and need the
`score_override` permission.

## A team legitimately burned all their guesses. Now what?

Open the challenge in the editor — its **Guesses** section restores
attempts for one team/competitor or for everyone. History is kept, so the
reset is visible and non-destructive.

## Pause or stop — which do I want?

**Pause** for incidents: submissions close, everyone sees the banner, the
schedule keeps its dates, and staff can still submit to verify a fix.
**Stop** ends gameplay. If an incident ate meaningful time, pause, fix,
then extend the end time in Controls before resuming.

## When can I safely test challenges myself?

Before the start and while paused — competitors are gated then, but staff
submissions work, so you can run the whole board end-to-end. Make the
dry-run a ritual: every attachment downloads, every target answers, every
flag submits.

## Import said it skipped some challenges.

The importer reports created / skipped / errors per run: challenges that
already exist are **skipped, never overwritten**, and per-file errors name
the offending entry. Fix the named entries and re-import — the already-
created ones simply skip.
