## Announcements

**New announcement** on the dashboard reaches everyone in the competition —
live as a banner on every open session, then in the archive and the bell.
Three controls matter:

- **Urgency** — *Announcement* sits in the banner briefly; *Important*
  stands out; *Urgent* stays until dismissed and reaches even competitors
  who muted announcements. Use it for incidents.
- **Send to** — everyone, specific teams, or specific competitors.
- **Schedule for later** — pick a **Publish time** and it stays a private
  draft until then. Pending posts wait in the dashboard's **Scheduled
  announcements** card (Reschedule, Publish now, Cancel) — queue the hint
  drops and challenge waves before the event starts.

Announce *before* competitors notice, not after the tickets arrive.

## The support queue

![A support ticket](assets/tickets.png)
*Tickets are live conversations — replies stream in real time on both sides,
and staff have their own tools alongside.*

Competitor tickets arrive in **Support**, with the open count on your
dashboard. In a thread: **Assign to me** claims it, **Internal note** keeps a
reply from the competitor, **Staff notes** is a live pad for staff only, and
**Resolve** closes it. Competitors see *A judge is looking at this ticket*
while you have it open.

Working principles: **acknowledge fast, resolve second**; **reproduce before
you rule** ("the flag won't submit" is a claim — check it in a safe place);
if a challenge really is broken, **pause or unpublish it**, announce, fix,
then consider the scoring impact; and resolve tickets so the queue reflects
reality.

## Questions and clarifications

On a programming problem, competitors can **Ask the judges** privately. The
question lands in Support tagged **Question** — one message you answer with
**Send answer** (or a quick answer: *No comment*, *Read the problem
statement*); they can't reply, only ask again. If the answer helps everyone,
tick **Also send to everyone as a clarification**: edit *The question, as
everyone will see it* — strip anything that gives away who asked or how
they're solving it — and **Send to everyone**. The same checkbox works on
any ticket about a challenge.

You can also **Post a clarification** straight from a challenge, and
**Withdraw** one later: it stays listed, marked withdrawn, with its answer
removed everywhere. Clarifications only go out while the competition is
running and the challenge is visible.

## Guess resets and awards

When a competitor legitimately exhausts their multiple-choice guesses, open
the challenge in the editor: **Guesses** offers *Reset for selected* or
*Reset for everyone* — history is kept. In an individual-mode competition,
**Participants → Create award** grants a titled award with points (zero for
a badge, negative to deduct); in team mode, credit a team through an
automation's *Create award* or *Update score* action.

!!! note "Fairness travels in pairs"
    Anything you grant one team — a reset, a nudge, extra time on a target —
    be prepared to grant every team in the same situation. The dashboard's
    **Moderation feed** shows recent score changes and deletions, and the
    administrator's event log records everything; post-event write-ups read
    better when the answer to "why them?" is "same as everyone".
